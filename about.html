OUR STORY

Built by Sales Leaders, for Sales Leaders

BPO Hive was founded in 2019 with a simple mission: to help B2B companies scale their sales operations without the overhead of building large in-house teams.

Our founders experienced firsthand the challenges of generating consistent, high-quality leads. They built BPO Hive to be the partner they wished they had—one that combines deep industry expertise with cutting-edge technology and a genuine commitment to client success.

Today, we're proud to serve over 1,000 clients across 50+ industries, delivering millions of qualified leads and helping businesses achieve their growth ambitions.
