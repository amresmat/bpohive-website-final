Questions brokers ask

What counts as a qualified interview?

A licensed agent who meets the criteria you set with us and agreed to a time on your calendar to talk about joining your brokerage.

Where do the agents come from?

We build the agent list for your market ourselves, then our team calls them.

Is there a contract?

No long contract. The program is month to month: $799 a month plus a one-time $500 setup fee.

What if you book fewer than 10 in a month?

The missing bookings are credited to the next month until we deliver them.

Can I hear the calls?

Yes. Every booking comes with our call notes and the call recording, so you hear exactly what the agent said.

Do you guarantee hires?

No. We do the finding, screening and booking. Whether an agent joins depends on your offer and the interview.

How many show up?

About 70% or higher, across more than 15,000 appointments we have booked for real estate leaders and broker owners.

Which markets do you cover?

Markets across the United States. Tell us where you recruit and we confirm on the call.
