What companies ask before starting.

Does BPO Hive provide the prospect data?

Yes, for B2B campaigns. We define the ICP and prepare prospect data around the approved company, market, and decision-maker criteria.

What is a touchpoint?

One outbound call, email, LinkedIn connection request, or LinkedIn message delivered as part of the campaign strategy.

What happens during the Validation Sprint?

We test the ICP, data, messaging, qualification, and channel mix through 10,000 touchpoints, then review what should be refined or scaled.

What can I see in the client platform?

Your dedicated login provides visibility into campaign activity, connections, conversations, qualified interest, and booked meetings.

Where are meetings booked?

Qualified prospects who express genuine interest are booked directly on your calendar, with relevant context handed to your sales team.

Are appointments or revenue guaranteed?

No. Outcomes depend on the offer, market, audience, timing, data, and sales process. BPO Hive provides transparent execution, reporting, and evidence-based optimization.
