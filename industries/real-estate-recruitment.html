Most Brokerages Don’t Have a Recruiting Problem.
They Have a Pipeline Problem.

You may already know the type of agents you want to bring in. The real challenge is reaching enough of them consistently, filtering for fit, and getting qualified interviews on the calendar — without overwhelming your internal team.

Relying on referrals alone is unpredictable. Job posts often bring noise instead of quality. Internal recruiters can get stuck spending too much time sourcing, chasing, screening, and following up.

That is where BPO Hive comes in.

We help real estate companies build a more reliable recruiting engine by booking pre-qualified agent interviews based on the exact standards they want us to work from.
